## Exact calls and matched treatment

All calls use `gpt-5.6-luna`, medium reasoning, web disabled,
`CODEX_HOME=/home/pineapple/.codex-new-account`, and one complete-track call.
Agent and evaluation caps are each 30 minutes.

Exactly five calls are allowed in the unchanged order: Plain, Graph, ordinary
Graph-feedback repair, generic verified-union adjudication, and semantic-
overlap verified-union adjudication. There are no per-task calls, retries,
top-ups, result-led prompt changes, or replacement candidates.

The two final arms use the same fixed verified-candidate priority (ordinary,
Graph, Plain), byte-identical verified union, byte restoration after the call,
candidate patches, outcomes, bounded failure evidence, model, effort, call
cap, and one final call. The TOV treatment is the unresolved-task ledger over
fault location, violated invariant, counterexample class, and edit intent,
disagreement-triggered falsification, and two completion audits.

## Frozen analysis

Report all 34 outcomes, calls, tokens, runtime, paired rescues/harms, exact
paired tests, and paired bootstrap intervals.

The fixed-sequence confirmatory gate is:

1. TOV must beat Plain by at least 7/34 tasks (+20.6 percentage points), with
   a one-sided exact paired p-value below .05.
2. Conditional on gate 1, TOV must have more rescues than harms versus ordinary
   repair.
3. Conditional on gates 1–2, TOV must beat the matched generic verified-union
   arm by at least 2/34 tasks (+5.9 points), with more rescues than harms.
4. Every verified anchor must be byte-identical at evaluation, the ledger IDs
   and order must exactly equal the unresolved task list, and all changed paths
   must be allowed solution files.

Gate 3 is a directional replication threshold, not an assurance of an
individually significant 34-task matched contrast. Report its exact p-value
without inflation. If Rust30 and Python34 both meet gate 3 with no matched
harms, report the pre-specified cumulative paired exact test across their
disjoint 64 tasks, explicitly labeling Rust30 developmental and the combined
analysis cumulative rather than pure confirmation. Additional language tracks
may be evaluated only as complete official tracks under a separately frozen,
unchanged-policy extension; no result-led task selection is allowed.

Infrastructure is provenance only. The potential paper contribution is the
end-to-end anchored TOV decision mechanism and its accuracy effect.
