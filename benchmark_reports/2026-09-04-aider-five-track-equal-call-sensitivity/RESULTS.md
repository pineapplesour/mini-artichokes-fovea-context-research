# Five-track equal-call Aider portfolio sensitivity

## Scope

This is a derived, descriptive sensitivity over the already completed five
complete Aider language tracks at commit
`7e0611e77b54e2dea774cdc0aa00cf9f7ed6144f`. It adds no model call and removes
no task. The compared systems both contain Plain, Graph, ordinary repair, and
semantic-free Direct. Their fifth route is Generic for the non-overlap
portfolio or TOV for Mini Artichokes. Each system therefore contains five
nominal whole-track model calls and the same per-task verifier-union rule.

The evidence stages are heterogeneous. Rust30/Python34/C++26 use the original
post-hoc recursive-union analysis, Go39 uses the model-complete post-primary
Generic replacement sensitivity, and JavaScript49 is the clean prospective
comparison. Consequently, the total below is not a new confirmatory endpoint.

## Complete-task result

| Track | Direct union Generic | Direct union TOV | Mini rescue:harm | Net |
|:---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Rust30 | 19/30 | 19/30 | 0:0 | 0 |
| Python34 | 21/34 | 23/34 | 2:0 | +2 |
| C++26 | 14/26 | 17/26 | 3:0 | +3 |
| Go39 replacement sensitivity | 31/39 | 33/39 | 3:1 | +2 |
| JavaScript49 prospective | 47/49 | 47/49 | 0:0 | 0 |
| **All 178 tasks** | **132/178 (74.16%)** | **139/178 (78.09%)** | **8:1** | **+7 (+3.93 pp)** |

On the nine discordant tasks, the exact one-sided paired binomial/McNemar
value is `.01953125` and the two-sided value is `.0390625`. A 50,000-draw
paired task bootstrap with NumPy `default_rng(20260904)` gives a 95% interval
of `[+1.12,+7.30]` percentage points. These task-level values condition on the
realized whole-track calls. At the complete-track level, Mini has positive net
differences on Python, C++, and Go and ties on Rust and JavaScript; a one-sided
sign test over the three nonzero track differences is `.125`.

## Interpretation

The sensitivity shows that the overlap-containing portfolio is not merely
being compared with one- or two-call baselines: against a five-call generic
portfolio it is seven tasks higher over the complete Aider inventory, with no
track-level net loss. It is still not prospective population evidence because
three source tracks informed the analysis and the Go Generic value is a
post-primary replacement. JavaScript, the clean prospective track, is an exact
tie. The systems are matched on nominal calls and hard caps, not on realized
tokens, latency, or monetary cost.

This result supports a bounded descriptive claim about the realized Aider
portfolio. It does not establish a universal TOV effect, compute efficiency,
or superiority to contemporary full external repair agents.
